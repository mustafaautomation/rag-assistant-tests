# Electric bikes

E-bikes have a blue frame and a battery under the rear rack. The assist works up to 25 km/h, after which you pedal without help.

The battery level is shown on the handlebar display and in the app before you unlock. A full battery gives about 60 km of assisted riding, less in cold weather or on steep hills.

Do not start a ride with less than 20 percent battery. The app will warn you and suggest another bike.

E-bike batteries are swapped by the Harbourline service team. Riders must never open the battery cover. Charging stations at docks are not available.
