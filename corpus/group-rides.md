# Group rides

One account can unlock up to four bikes at the same time for a group ride. Start the first ride as usual, then choose Add rider and scan the next bike.

All rides in a group are charged to the account holder. Pass benefits apply only to the bike the account holder rides; the other bikes pay the single-ride price.

The account holder is responsible for every bike in the group until each one is returned to a dock.

Group rides are not available for e-bikes at the moment.
