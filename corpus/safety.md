# Riding safely

Riders must be at least 16 years old. Riders under 18 need a parent or guardian to accept the terms in the app.

Helmets are not provided but are strongly recommended. Every bike has front and rear lights that switch on automatically when you start pedalling.

Riding on footpaths is not allowed unless a sign says it is a shared path. Follow the same traffic rules as other cyclists.

Only one person may ride a bike at a time. Carrying passengers, including children, is not allowed, and child seats cannot be fitted.
