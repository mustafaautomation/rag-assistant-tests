# Accessibility

The Harbourline app supports screen readers on iOS and Android, and all text can be enlarged with the system font size.

Adaptive tricycles are available at three stations: Central Quay, Riverside Park and the University stop. They must be booked in the app at least 2 hours in advance.

A carer riding with a rider who uses an adaptive tricycle rides free of charge. Add the carer in the booking.

If a station is not step-free, the station page in the app says so.
