# Winter and bad weather

The service runs all year. During snow or ice warnings, e-bikes are taken out of service, and classic bikes stay available unless the city closes the bike lanes.

When wind speeds above 70 km/h are forecast, all stations are closed and no new rides can start. Rides already in progress can still be ended at any dock.

Closures are announced in the app and by push notification at least 2 hours ahead when possible.

Membership time is not extended for weather closures.
