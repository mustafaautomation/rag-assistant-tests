# Pricing and passes

Harbourline Bikes offers three ways to pay. A single ride costs 2.50 for the first 30 minutes on a classic bike. Every extra 15 minutes costs 1.00.

A day pass costs 9.00 and covers unlimited rides of up to 45 minutes each for 24 hours from the first unlock. Rides longer than 45 minutes on a day pass are charged 1.00 per extra 15 minutes.

The annual membership costs 79.00 per year. Members get unlimited 45-minute rides on classic bikes and a 50 percent discount on e-bike unlock fees.

E-bikes always add an unlock fee of 1.00 plus 0.15 per minute, on top of the pass or single-ride price.

Students with a valid student card can buy the annual membership for 49.00. The discount is verified once a year in the app.
