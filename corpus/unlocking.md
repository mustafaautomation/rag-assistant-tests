# Unlocking and returning a bike

To unlock a bike, scan the QR code on the handlebar with the app, or type the five-digit bike number. The dock light turns green and releases the bike within a few seconds.

To end a ride, push the bike firmly into any free dock until the light turns green and you hear two beeps. The ride ends only when the dock confirms the return. The app shows a ride summary within one minute.

If a station is full, the app shows the nearest station with free docks. Open Ride, then Station full, to get 15 extra minutes at no charge while you find a dock.

Bikes left outside a dock for more than 24 hours are treated as lost, and a fee of 150.00 may be charged.
