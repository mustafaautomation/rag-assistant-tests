# Reporting a damaged bike

If a bike has a problem, do not ride it. In the app, open Report, choose the bike number and select the problem, such as a flat tyre, broken brake, loose saddle or faulty light.

After you report a bike, dock it and press the red wrench button on the dock for two seconds. This marks the bike as out of service so the next rider cannot take it.

If a bike breaks during a ride, end the ride at the nearest station and report it within 30 minutes. Any charge for the remaining time of that ride is refunded automatically after the report is checked.

You are not charged for normal wear. A damage fee applies only when damage is caused on purpose or by clear misuse.
