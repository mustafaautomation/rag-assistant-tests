# Managing your membership

Annual memberships renew automatically on the anniversary of the purchase date. You will get a reminder email 14 days before renewal.

To cancel automatic renewal, open the app, go to Account, then Membership, and switch off Auto-renew. Your membership stays active until the end of the paid year.

If you cancel within 14 days of buying or renewing, and you have taken no rides in that period, you get a full refund. After 14 days, or once you have ridden, the membership is not refundable.

A membership belongs to one person and cannot be shared. Each member can unlock one bike at a time. To ride with a friend, see the article on group rides.
