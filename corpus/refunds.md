# Refunds and disputes

If you think a ride was charged wrongly, open the ride in your history and choose Dispute charge within 30 days of the ride. Disputes are answered within 5 working days.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds can take up to 10 working days to appear, depending on your bank.

Common reasons for a refund include a dock that did not confirm the return, a bike that broke during the ride, and a duplicate charge.

Ride credits that were given as a goodwill gesture expire after 12 months and cannot be exchanged for cash.
