# Signing in and account security

You sign in with your email address and a one-time code. The code is valid for 10 minutes. Harbourline never asks for a password.

If you do not receive the code, check your spam folder and wait one minute before requesting a new one. After five failed codes, sign-in is blocked for 30 minutes.

You can be signed in on up to three devices. Signing in on a fourth device signs out the oldest one.

Change your email address under Account, then Profile. A confirmation code is sent to both the old and the new address.
