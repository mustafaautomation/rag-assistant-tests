# Your data and privacy

Harbourline stores the start and end station, time and distance of every ride. GPS location is recorded only while a ride is in progress.

Ride history is kept for 24 months and then deleted. Payment records are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it.

You can download a copy of your data from Account, then Privacy, then Export my data. The file is ready within 48 hours.

To delete your account, choose Delete account on the same screen. Any active membership ends without a refund, unless it is still within the 14-day refund period.
